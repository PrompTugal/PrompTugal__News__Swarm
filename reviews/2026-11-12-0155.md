## 3. The assurance buyer

**Lands**
1. **"What this policy does not cover yet"** as an on-stage card: Unix sockets by path, inherited environment variables, UDP. Saying the limits out loud is what earns trust.
2. **PKI scene:** fingerprints on screen: packet `sha256:76bec41cb73435ca` and runner `sha256:aedceeff1292aae0`. Two runners of one packet are told apart by their keys.
3. **The handover (relay.1 → relay.2 → relay.3)** puts each step in one of four places: `git-public`, `vault-encrypted`, `memory-decrypted`, `network`. That is exactly the question a CISO asks ("where is it in plaintext?"). The task's `signed_by: sha256:8d8a0fd4e656c3ae` matches relay.1's `signing_fingerprint` in handover/keys.json, so it checks out.

**Confuses or worries**
1. **"physics" and "no access to your systems" overclaim.** The Action holds things: at least the router key (it calls OpenRouter), a key to sign its result, and whatever token lets it commit `result.json`. It cannot reach Gmail because it holds *no Gmail credential and no connector*, not because of physics. A CISO will ask "what does the Action hold?", and the stage does not say.
2. **h001 step 2 says `memory-decrypted`, but its text says "holds the input on its own disk".** Plaintext on disk is the weak point a CISO looks for, and the label hides it. Step 3 (put into a vault and push) is labelled **A1** (a model call), but no model is involved: it is A0. Step 5 has `signed_by: null`, although the task file *is* signed (`8d8a0fd4…`).
3. **"Shredded" and "lived only for its run".** h001 step 4 says "the plaintext key is shredded". The scale scene says the key "lived only for its run". Neither cites evidence, which storytelling.1 flagged too. Claims of destruction need a log line, or softer words.

**One fix (swarm.1.dev with storytelling.1, scene `elsewhere`):** replace "Not a policy: physics." with what the Action actually holds, for example: "It holds three things: the router key, its own repository, a key for this run. Gmail is not one of them." Use the true list from the Runs workflow. Relay.3 / relay.1: in flows/h001.json, set step 2 to `disk-plaintext` (or add that to FORMAT.json's `where`), set step 3 to A0, and set step 5's `signed_by`.

## What works for all three

- No horizontal scroll at 390 or 1920 on either page; no console errors.
- Labels are honest: "(planned)", "(potential)", "Next.", "Not measured: GitHub queue time … the requester agent's own Claude tokens". That habit is the strongest thing on the site; every reader below leans on it.

## 1. The engineer (a principal engineer or CTO)

**Lands**
1. **Real runs, replayable.** Clicking r5b gives a timeline of 51.0 s, 48 loops, 73 LLM calls, peak 96 units at once, a per-model table (calls, valid, tokens, USD, median ms) and the run file one link away. It is checkable, not a slide.
2. **The failure is kept.** r4c-try1 "(out of tokens)" sits in the ladder: 32 calls, 3 valid (`runs/20261111T2257-r4c.json`), next to r4c at 12 of 12. Showing the bad run is what makes the good one believable.
3. **The SHarness evidence is measured, not designed.** `harness/…probe-and-hello.json` records the "before" (uid 0, all capabilities, `no_new_privs` 0, seccomp 0) and 6 of 6 probes held under one deny-all policy, with a `not_covered` list (Unix sockets by path, inherited environment variables, UDP). Naming what is not covered is exactly right.

**Confuses or worries**
1. **r5b validity is low and not said aloud.** The page shows "73 · 39 valid". qwen3.7-flash: 30 calls, 3 valid, median 15,360 ms. The story's scene 5.3 quotes "48 loops, 73 calls, peak 96" but not "39 valid". An engineer will find it in five seconds and wonder what else was trimmed.
2. **"Consensus" with 1 of 4.** In r5b's consensus table, `trains: disrupted, 1 of 4` and `ferries: disrupted, 2 of 4`, `forecast: normal, 2 of 4`. One model of four is not consensus; it reads as a bug in the rule.
3. **The first view is empty.** The page opens on r0: the agent node `swarm.1 (Claude)` is a near-invisible dark square with an unreadable label, the timeline reads "beginning · 0 s … end · 0.0 s", and the tiles are all 0 / $0.0000. The first impression of "the swarm" is a blank run.

**One fix (swarm.1.dev, index.html):** in the r5b consensus table, show "no consensus" when fewer than 3 of 4 agree (or say the rule in one line under the table: "most common valid answer; ties and 1-of-4 shown as weak"), and put the valid share in the headline tile ("73 calls · 39 valid (53%)").

## 2. The investor

**Lands**
1. **The cost staircase is the memorable number.** Same r5b tokens (175,715 in, 60,190 out): Opus $1.91, Sonnet $0.95, Haiku $0.048, OpenRouter $0.031 (`metrics.potential_claude_usd`, `openrouter_usd`). That is about a 62× gap between Opus and what it actually cost (1.9067 ÷ 0.0308, computed by me from those two figures), and it is all from one file.
2. **"1 push → 12 jobs · 144 calls · $0.039 · 47 s"** in the headline band is the scale story in one chip.
3. **The story draft has one sentence:** "Don't hope the agent behaves. Make the dangerous thing unreachable." That is the wedge.

**Confuses or worries**
1. **Two Sonnet numbers on one screen.** The headline band says "$1.99 same tokens on Claude Sonnet" (the 15 push runs, 630,970 tokens). Below, r5b's tile says "$0.9533 … Sonnet" and "$1.9067 … Opus". A viewer reading quickly sees "$1.99 Sonnet" above "$1.91 Opus" and concludes Sonnet costs more than Opus.
2. **The problem is not on the page.** index.html opens with "The swarm, one real run at a time" and the ladder. The problem (every agent holds every connector; reach, not behaviour, is the control) lives only in the story draft and the "Who holds what" table at the bottom of how.html. In 30 seconds an investor learns *what was run*, not *why it matters*.
3. **"Next." four times.** Of the four demos listed for Thursday, three say "Next." (sessions across accounts, clouds, where the enterprise looks). Honest, but it makes the box read as a to-do list rather than a plan.

**One fix (storytelling.1, the opening of stage.html / present mode):** open on the problem, not the ladder: one slide with the sentence above and one number. Suggested number: the r5b staircase line, "same work: $1.91 on Opus, $0.031 as we run it". And label the headline band's Sonnet figure with its scope ("15 push runs, 630,970 tokens") so it is never read next to r5b's.

## 3. The assurance buyer (a CISO or risk lead)

**Lands**
1. **Mandate, grant and budget are in the packet**, and signed (how.html, step 2: "a mandate (what to do), a grant (what the runner may touch …) and a budget"). The budget is written before the run and shown against spend: r5b "$0.0308 of $0.5000", "73 of 144" calls, "Router credit before the run: $70.86".
2. **"Who holds what" with a *Cannot* column** (how.html): an A1 call "cannot use any tool"; a loop cannot "start others, schedule, push". That table is the control model in five rows.
3. **Attestation anyone can check:** `gh attestation verify runs/<id>/result.json -R PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Swarm__Runs` (how.html). Model text never stored; only length, sha256, valid.

**Confuses or worries**
1. **"Cannot" — enforced by what?** The how.html table does not say whether each *Cannot* is enforced by the platform (no credential, no network, a sandbox) or by the agent's own code. The story itself says (scene 3.2) "a policy the agent enforces on itself is still hope". A CISO will ask this of every row.
2. **Per-call keys, but who vouches for them?** "73 of 73 LLM calls (A1) signed, each with a key made for that call (73 keys)". Fresh keys are good, but the page does not say how a verifier knows those 73 keys belong to this run (signed by the runner's key? listed in the result?). `data/keys.json` lists the swarm.1 session key, which "ends with the session" — so how is a run verified next week?
3. **The harness evidence is invisible.** 6 of 6 probes held, root with all capabilities before: the most reassuring fact in the repository is only in a JSON file. Also the `hash` probe reads "got: refused, exit 125" with no explanation of what was hashed.

**One fix (swarm.1.dev with swarm.1.designer, how.html "Who holds what"):** add a column "Enforced by" with one of: *no credential*, *no network (SHarness policy, hash `sha256:7db87b…`)*, *GitHub permissions*, *the runner's code (hope)*. Be honest where it is still the code. Link the harness JSON from that column.

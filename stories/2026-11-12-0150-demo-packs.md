## The strongest evidence for mandate, reach, gap (checked)

Fringe `data/run-reach.json` (architect.1, 2026-11-11T15:40Z) measures one real run, unit by unit: what each **needed** against what it **held**, in capability primitives.

| Unit | Altitude | Needed | Held | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the agent (Claude Code session) | A3 | 14 | 25 | 11 |
| compute: plan (node from the agent's shell) | A0 | 3 | 25 | **22** |
| loop: 3 sub-agents running node | A2 | 5 | 25 | 20 |
| 81 LLM calls (text in, text out, no tools) | A1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| compute: plan, **narrowed** (`node --permission`) | A0 | 3 | 4 | **1** |

Its answer, verbatim: "Because it was started from the agent's shell: a process inherits its parent's whole reach. A0 plan needed 3 primitives and held 25. Narrowed with Node's permission model it holds 4 ... Only the LLM calls were at their altitude: text in, text out."

*Idea, the scene:* five bars, needed against held. The code unit that needed 3 and held 25 is the "poof" from the long story's act 2, with real data. Then the narrowed bar drops to 4: the gap closed from 22 to 1. This is also why the long story's act 3.3 works: an LLM call through the router has a gap of 0.

## The pack every other pack leans on: Mandate, reach, gap

*Idea:* tell this one first. It gives the audience three words they'll use for the rest of the demo.

- **Mandate:** what a unit is allowed to do. **Fact:** every packet holds "a mandate (what to do), a grant (what the runner may touch: the storm API, the router, one file, a push to dev) and a budget (USD and calls)" (how.html, step 2).
- **Reach:** what the process running it can actually touch. **Fact:** in swarm.1's container, before any policy: uid 0, all capabilities, no seccomp, no no_new_privs, `iptables`, `useradd` and `strace` reachable, the working directory writable, "the whole internet through the session proxy" (harness/2026-11-12-swarm1-probe-and-hello.json, E01).
- **Gap:** reach minus mandate. That's where things go wrong.

| # | The one point | Form | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An LLM call's mandate is one line: a prompt in, an answer out. | slide | how.html, "Who holds what": an A1 call "cannot use any tool" |
| 2 | The process it ran in could reach: root, every capability, the whole internet. | animated: the reach circle grows around a tiny mandate dot | harness file, E01 |
| 3 | The space between the two is the gap. Every incident lives there. | animated: the ring between dot and circle turns red | *idea* |
| 4 | Close the gap two ways: shrink the reach (the harness, pack 3), or move the work where the reach was never there (pack 2). | slide | *idea* |

| Listener | The line (*idea*) |
|---|---|
| Engineer | "Your agent's process has root and the internet. Its job needed neither." |
| Investor | "Every agent incident so far fits in the gap between what it was asked and what it could do. We measure the gap and close it." |
| CISO | "Mandate is policy. Reach is fact. Audit the gap, not the prompt." |

## The pack every other pack leans on: Mandate, reach, gap

*Idea:* tell this one first. It gives the audience three words they'll use for the rest of the demo.

- **Mandate:** what a unit is allowed to do. **Fact:** every packet holds "a mandate (what to do), a grant (what the runner may touch: the storm API, the router, one file, a push to dev) and a budget (USD and calls)" (how.html, step 2).
- **Reach:** what the process running it can actually touch. **Fact:** in swarm.1's container, before any policy: uid 0, all capabilities, no seccomp, no no_new_privs, `iptables`, `useradd` and `strace` reachable, the working directory writable, "the whole internet through the session proxy" (harness/2026-11-12-swarm1-probe-and-hello.json, E01).
- **Gap:** reach minus mandate. That's where things go wrong.
